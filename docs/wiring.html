Wiring Reference

ESP32-WROOM-32 DIY ExpressLRS handset — power distribution and every peripheral connection, color-coded by net. Pin numbers are the ESP32 GPIO.

Power distribution

INA226 is high-side on the main + rail: IN+ = supply, IN− = load (reversing only flips the current sign). GND is common to the pack, module, ESP32, TFT, sensors, and pots.

Net colors

VBAT 7–13 V 3V3 GND CRSF / UART SPI (TFT) I²C ADC (gimbals)

Connections

ELRS module (CRSF)

UART2 · half-duplex

GPIO16→CRSF pad
pack +→7–13 V

Both pins meet at the one CRSF pad. Non-inverted 3.3 V — no level shifter.

MISO unused. 76×284 bar panel, rotation 1.

MCP23017

I²C · 0x20
GPIO21→SDA
GPIO22→SCL
A0 / A1 / A2→GND (addr 0x20)
RESET→3V3 (hold high)
VDD→3V3
VSS→GND

GPA0–7 + GPB0–1 drive the 10 switches (below). RESET must stay high.

INA226

I²C · 0x40
GPIO21→SDA
GPIO22→SCL
A0 / A1→GND (addr 0x40)
IN+ / IN−↔across shunt
VS→3V3

Shunt = two 0.05 Ω 2512 in parallel = 0.025 Ω → ~3.28 A FS. One 4.7 kΩ I²C pull-up pair for the whole bus.

Gimbals (pots)

Each wiper: 1 kΩ series + 100 nF to GND (RC low-pass) into the ADC. GPIO32 dead, GPIO39 spare.
